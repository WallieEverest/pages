Install on iPhone or iPad
  1. Open an app above (or this page) in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Boating and Slideshow: keep Open as Web App on (they open full screen). Manager and My Apps: turn it off (they open in Safari). Tap Add.
  4. An icon added before keeps its old mode: delete it and add it again.
